I'm JMO. I build weird stuff: from laser-cut chaos to reverse-engineered
gadgets. I film it all from my garage, powered by synthwave, caffeine, and
curiosity. If it doesn't exist yet, that's just a challenge I haven't
accepted yet.

## The things I make are Fragments

One person, a lot of small self-contained projects. Each one is a Fragment,
and together they're Fragments. Some are physical, some are code, most end up
being a bit of both.

What I spend my time on:

- **Hardware reverse engineering.** Sub-GHz radio, NFC, RFID, infrared, and
  the tooling to drive all of it. Mostly the pleasure of finding out what a
  device is actually saying when it thinks nobody is listening.
- **Wearables and strange interfaces.** Smart glasses, heads-up displays, and
  putting screens on things that have no business having one.
- **Self-hosted everything.** Phone apps, calendars, home automation, running
  on hardware I own with no cloud in the middle. If I can't unplug it from the
  internet and still have it work, I'm not done.
- **Things that are actually physical.** 3D printing, a 100W laser cutter,
  ESP32s, too many LEDs, and the occasional cursed gadget.

## Where the builds actually show up

**[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jmomakesstuff/) is the best place to
follow along.** That's where builds land first, usually while they're still
covered in sawdust and not entirely working.

The bigger ones get written up at
[jmomakesstuff.com](https://www.jmomakesstuff.com/) and filmed for
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@JMOMakesStuff), where there's room for the
process, the failures, and the parts that didn't work the first four times.

Public code is starting to land on this account. If it looks quiet here, it
isn't. Most of it is just still in the garage.
